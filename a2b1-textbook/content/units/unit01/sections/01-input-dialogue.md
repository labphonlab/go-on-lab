## Input：対話を聞いてみましょう

音声を聞きながら、Ben と Aya が週末の予定を立てる様子を読みましょう。

**Ben:** Hi, Aya! Are you free this weekend?

**Aya:** Hi, Ben! Yes, I think so. Why?

**Ben:** I'm going to go to the new museum on Saturday. Do you want to join?

**Aya:** That sounds great! What time are you going to go?

**Ben:** Maybe about ten in the morning. Is that okay?

**Aya:** Sure! Actually, I'm busy in the morning. Can we meet up later?

**Ben:** Okay, no problem. Shall we meet at one o'clock?

**Aya:** Great. And after the museum, why don't we go to the park?

**Ben:** Good idea! It's probably going to be sunny on Saturday.

**Aya:** Great. I'll bring some food, and we can eat in the park.

**Ben:** Sounds good. Should I invite Mika, too?

**Aya:** Yes, definitely! I'll text her tonight.

**Ben:** Okay. Oh wait, I have a birthday party on Saturday night. I can't stay too late.

**Aya:** No problem. We'll finish before that.

**Ben:** Great, see you at one, then!

**Aya:** See you then! I'm looking forward to it.

---

### 内容チェック

1. Ben and Aya are going to meet on ( Saturday / Sunday ).
2. They are going to go to the museum, and then the ( park / beach ).
3. Ben has a ( birthday party / concert ) on Saturday night.
