音声を聞きながら、Jack と Yuna が先週の出来事について話す様子を読みましょう。

**Yuna:** Hi, Jack! How was your week?

**Jack:** Hi, Yuna! It was busy, but very good. How about you?

**Yuna:** Not bad. Tell me about your week. What did you do first?

**Jack:** Okay, on Monday I had a big meeting with my boss and a colleague. We talked about a new project.

**Yuna:** That sounds busy. Did you finish it?

**Jack:** Not yet. We have a deadline next month. After the meeting, I worked until six.

**Yuna:** Oh! What about Tuesday?

**Jack:** On Tuesday, I cleaned the house in the morning. Then I did the laundry and the dishes.

**Yuna:** That's so much work! Did you do anything exciting?

**Jack:** Yes! After that, I went shopping and bought some groceries. Then I made dinner.

**Yuna:** Nice! What did you do next?

**Jack:** On Wednesday, I had an appointment with the doctor. Then I met a friend for coffee.

**Yuna:** That's nice. And Thursday?

**Jack:** On Thursday, I visited my grandmother. I brought her some groceries, too. My cousin was there.

**Yuna:** Nice! What about Friday?

**Jack:** On Friday, after work, I played tennis with a colleague. Then I relaxed at home and saw a movie.

**Yuna:** Sounds relaxing! And the weekend?

**Jack:** On Saturday, I studied and reviewed for a test. Finally, on Sunday, I took a short trip with my family. We had a great time.

**Yuna:** Oh, your week sounds exciting! My week was boring. I just did some chores and got a haircut.

**Jack:** Sometimes boring is good! I'm exhausted now.

**Yuna:** I understand! Let's catch up again next week.

**Jack:** Okay! Sounds great.

---

### 内容チェック

1. Jack had a meeting on ( Monday / Tuesday ).
2. Jack visited his grandmother on ( Wednesday / Thursday ).
3. On Sunday, Jack ( took a trip / played tennis ) with his family.
