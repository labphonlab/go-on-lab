  scenario: >
    The learner and an AI friend named Mia are catching up about last week.
    Mia already knows what she did every day last week (in order); the
    learner does not know these details and must ask questions to find out
    what Mia did, then compare it with their own week.
  ai_role: >
    You are Mia, a friendly peer of the learner. You already have a full
    week of events last week (for example: a meeting on Monday, cleaning on
    Tuesday, a doctor's appointment on Wednesday, visiting a relative on
    Thursday, playing a sport on Friday, and a trip on the weekend). Do not
    reveal your whole week at once -- answer one question at a time, and let
    the learner ask about specific days. Respond only using vocabulary the
    learner has already studied (see known_vocab_cap). Use natural, short
    A2-B1 level turns, and use simple past tense correctly as a model for
    the learner.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask Mia questions to find out what she did on at least three different
    days last week. Use sequencing words (first, then, after that, finally)
    to retell Mia's week back to her in order, and compare it with your own
    week using at least two simple past tense sentences.
  known_vocab_cap: unit_02
  recast: true
  clarification_requests: true
  eval_rubric: >
    1) Did the learner ask at least three questions about specific days or
    events to gather information from Mia? 2) Did the learner use at least

    the learner use at least two sequencing words (first, then, after that,
    finally) to organize events in order? 4) Did the learner compare Mia's
    week with their own week in at least one sentence?

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. 先週あなたが実際にしたことを3つ、順番に思い出しましょう（例：勉強した、友だちに会った、買い物に行った、など）。
2. Mia（AI の友だち）は、先週の自分の予定をすべて知っています。あなたは知らないので、質問して聞き出しましょう。
3. 次の表現を最低1回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - 過去形の動詞で、自分がしたことを伝える（例：*I visited my grandmother.*）
   - 曜日や出来事について質問する（例：*What did you do on Monday? Did you go anywhere last week?*）
   - **first / then / after that / finally** を使って、Mia の1週間を順番にまとめて話す

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/2` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Mia と英語でやり取りしながら、お互いの先週を比べてみましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

友だち役を自分で想像し、先週について質問し合うチャットのやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。**過去形の動詞**と、**first / then / after that / finally** のうち少なくとも2つを必ず使ってください。

```
You:
Friend:
You:
Friend:
You:
Friend:
```
