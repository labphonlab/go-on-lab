音声を聞きながら、Jack と Mei が自分の経験について話す様子を読みましょう。

**Jack:** Hi Mei! Have you ever traveled abroad?

**Mei:** Yes, I have! I traveled to Thailand with my family last year. Have you ever been abroad?

**Jack:** No, never. I have not traveled abroad yet, but I want to go one day.

**Mei:** You should! Have you ever eaten very spicy food?

**Jack:** Yes, I have! I have eaten very spicy curry many times. I love it. What about you?

**Mei:** Yes! In Thailand, I ate spicy soup every day. I have never eaten anything so spicy in my life.

**Jack:** Wow, that sounds amazing. Have you ever climbed a mountain?

**Mei:** Yes, once. My family and I climbed a small mountain. It was tiring, but the view was beautiful. Have you ever done something like that?

**Jack:** No, I have not. I have never climbed a mountain, but I have ridden a roller coaster many times. I love them.

**Mei:** Wow! I have never ridden a roller coaster. I think they look scary.

**Jack:** They are exciting, not scary! Have you ever seen a famous person?

**Mei:** Oh, yes! I saw a famous singer at the airport once. I was so surprised.

**Jack:** No way, that is amazing. I have never seen a famous person in my life.

**Mei:** I think you will, one day. Okay, I should go now. This was a great talk!

**Jack:** It was! Let's talk about this again next time.

---

### 内容チェック

1. Mei has traveled to ( Thailand / Korea ).
2. Jack has never ( climbed a mountain / ridden a roller coaster ).
3. Mei has seen a famous ( actor / singer ) at the airport.
