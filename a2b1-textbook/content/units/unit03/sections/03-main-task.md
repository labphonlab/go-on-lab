  scenario: >
    The learner meets an AI traveler named Nora at a hostel. Nora has a secret
    list of five unusual experiences (for example: she has been skydiving,
    she has never eaten sushi, she has ridden a camel, she has never seen
    snow, she has climbed a famous mountain). The learner does not know this
    list and must ask "Have you ever...?" questions to discover which
    experiences Nora has had and which she has not, then share three of
    their own experiences in return.
  ai_role: >
    You are Nora, a friendly traveler the learner just met. You privately
    know a fixed list of five experiences (some you have had, some you have
    never had) but you must not list them all at once -- reveal each one
    only when the learner asks a relevant "Have you ever...?" question.
    Answer naturally with "Yes, I have. I have ..." or "No, I have not. /
    No, never." and add one short detail. Respond only using vocabulary the
    learner has already studied (see known_vocab_cap). Use natural, short
    A2-B1 level turns, and ask the learner "Have you ever...?" questions
    back so the exchange feels balanced.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask Nora at least three "Have you ever...?" questions to find out about
    her experiences, and tell Nora about at least two of your own
    experiences using "I have..." or "I have never...".
  known_vocab_cap: unit_03
  recast: true
  clarification_requests: true
  eval_rubric: >
    1) Did the learner form at least three correct "Have you ever...?"
    questions with the past participle? 2) Did the learner use "have/has +
    past participle" at least twice to talk about their own experiences?
    3) Did the learner use "ever" and "never" correctly at least once each?
    4) Was the exchange a real two-way conversation (learner also answered

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. あなたが今までにしたことがある経験を3つ考えましょう（例：travel abroad, eat spicy food, climb a mountain など）。
2. Nora（AI の旅行者）は、あなたが知らない情報（Nora の経験）を持っています。**Have you ever ...?** と質問して聞き出しましょう。
3. 次の表現を最低2回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - **Have you ever 〜?** で経験をたずねる
   - **I have 〜 / I have never 〜** で自分の経験を伝える
   - 経験の回数を伝える表現（例：*once, many times*）

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/3` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Nora と英語でやり取りしながら、お互いの経験について話しましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

旅行者役を自分で想像し、経験について聞き合うやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。**Have you ever 〜?** と **I have 〜 / I have never 〜** を必ず使ってください。

```
You:
Nora:
You:
Nora:
You:
Nora:
```
