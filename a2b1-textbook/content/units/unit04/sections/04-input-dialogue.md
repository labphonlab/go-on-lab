音声を聞きながら、Ben と Aya が二つのアパートを比べる様子を読みましょう。

**Ben:** Hi Aya! Can you help me choose between two apartments?

**Aya:** Okay! Tell me about them.

**Ben:** The first one is close to the station, but it's expensive. The second one is far, but it's much cheaper.

**Aya:** How much cheaper?

**Ben:** About 8,000 yen a month cheaper. But the second one is smaller.

**Aya:** Is the kitchen small too?

**Ben:** Yes, the kitchen in the second one is smaller, but it's newer.

**Aya:** Is the first one noisy? I know you like a quiet place.

**Ben:** Yes, it's on a busy street, so it's noisier. The second one is quieter because it's in a small neighborhood.

**Aya:** That's good for you. Is the second one as convenient as the first one?

**Ben:** No. The first one is more convenient because it's closer to the supermarket and the station.

**Aya:** That's a difficult choice. Which one do you like better?

**Ben:** I think the second one is better for me. It's cheaper and quieter. The distance isn't a big problem.

**Aya:** Is the neighborhood safe?

**Ben:** Yes, it's safer than my street now. People say it's very friendly.

**Aya:** Great! When do you need to decide?

**Ben:** I need to decide before Friday. I'll visit both apartments again tomorrow.

**Aya:** I think the second one sounds like the best choice for you.

**Ben:** Thanks, Aya. You always help me decide.

---

### 内容チェック

1. Which apartment is closer to the station, the first one or the second one?
2. The second apartment is ( noisier / quieter ) than the first one.
3. Ben thinks the ( first / second ) apartment is better for him.
