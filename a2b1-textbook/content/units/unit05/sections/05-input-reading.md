Ben and Aya met at the park on Saturday. It was sunny, and they were both happy to see each other again.

"How was your week?" Ben asked.

"Busy!" Aya said. "I had a big project at work, and the deadline was on Friday. But I still visited my grandmother and played tennis with my cousin. What about you?"

"My week was good, too," Ben said. "Actually, I have a question. Have you ever climbed a mountain?"

"No, never," Aya said. "Why?"

"Because I climbed one on my trip to the island last year," Ben said. "It was one of the most exciting things I have ever done. The view from the top was amazing — even better than the view from the beach."

"Wow, that sounds great," Aya said. "I have never done anything like that. Maybe I should try it."

Later, they talked about their new apartments. "My apartment is quieter than yours," Ben said, "but I think your apartment is more convenient. It's closer to the station."

"That's true," Aya said. "Your kitchen is bigger than mine, though. I think our apartments are about as expensive as each other."

Then Jack came to the park with Yuna and Mei. "Hi, everyone!" Jack said. "Yuna, tell them about your trip."

"I recently went on a trip with my family," Yuna said. "We visited a famous festival, and I tasted a lot of amazing food. It was more relaxing than my usual busy week."

"And Mei has an interesting story, too," Jack said.

"Have you ever ridden a roller coaster?" Mei asked. "I first rode one last month. At first, I was scared, but it was actually really fun. I have never been more surprised in my life!"

Everyone laughed. It was a beautiful day, and they were happy to be together again.
