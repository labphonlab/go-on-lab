音声を聞きながら、旅行者の Leo が、地元の人 Grace に道を尋ねる様子を読みましょう。

**Leo:** Excuse me. Could you help me? I think I'm lost.

**Grace:** Okay! Where do you want to go?

**Leo:** I want to find the library. Do you know where it is?

**Grace:** Yes, it's not far from here. Go straight along this street.

**Leo:** Okay. Then what?

**Grace:** When you get to the traffic light, cross the street and turn left.

**Leo:** Cross at the traffic light, then turn left. Got it.

**Grace:** After that, walk past the bank and the supermarket.

**Leo:** Past the bank and the supermarket, okay.

**Grace:** The library is next to the post office, across from the park.

**Leo:** So it's near the park, right?

**Grace:** Yes, it's right across from it. It's easy to find.

**Leo:** Great, thank you! How many minutes is it?

**Grace:** About ten minutes. It's within walking distance.

**Leo:** Could you tell me if there is a pharmacy near here, too?

**Grace:** Yes, there is one behind the bank. It's opposite the gas station.

**Leo:** Behind the bank, opposite the gas station. Got it.

**Grace:** Would you like me to write down the directions for you?

**Leo:** Yes, please! That would be really helpful.

**Grace:** Here you go. Turn left at the corner, and the bookstore is in front of the station.

**Leo:** And the library?

**Grace:** Keep going, and you'll see it on your left. If you go past it, just turn around.

**Leo:** Thanks so much. You're very helpful.

**Grace:** No problem at all! Have a nice day.

**Leo:** You too. Bye!

---

### 内容チェック

1. Leo wants to find the ( library / bank ).
2. Grace tells Leo to ( turn right / turn left ) at the traffic light.
3. The pharmacy is behind the bank and opposite the ( gas station / bookstore ).
4. The whole walk takes about ( ten minutes / thirty minutes ).
