音声を聞きながら、Ben と Aya がレストランで注文する様子を読みましょう。

**Waiter:** Hi, welcome! Are you ready to order?

**Aya:** Yes, I think so. Could I have a look at the menu one more time?

**Waiter:** Of course, take your time.

**Ben:** Actually, we're ready. What's today's special?

**Waiter:** Today's special is chicken with rice and salad. It's really delicious.

**Aya:** That sounds nice. I'd like the chicken, please.

**Waiter:** I'm sorry, the chicken is sold out today. Would you like the steak instead?

**Aya:** Okay, I'll have the steak, then. Is it spicy?

**Waiter:** No, it's not spicy. You get bread and a small salad with it.

**Aya:** Great. Could I also have some soup to start?

**Waiter:** Sure. And for you?

**Ben:** I'd like a pizza, please. Could I have extra cheese on it?

**Waiter:** No problem. Would you like anything to drink?

**Ben:** Yes, could I have a coffee, please?

**Aya:** And a tea for me, please. No sugar.

**Waiter:** Are you allergic to anything?

**Ben:** No, we're okay, thanks.

**Waiter:** Great. Your food will be ready soon.

**Aya:** This is delicious!

**Ben:** Yes, I really like it. Could we have some more bread, please?

**Waiter:** Of course.

**Aya:** Could we have the bill, please?

**Waiter:** Sure, here you are. Was everything okay?

**Ben:** Yes, everything was great. Thank you!

**Waiter:** You're welcome. Have a good day!

---

### 内容チェック

1. The chicken is ( sold out / spicy ), so Aya orders the ( steak / sandwich ) instead.
2. Ben orders a pizza with extra ( cheese / sugar ).
3. At the end, Ben and Aya ask the waiter for the ( bill / menu ).
