今度は自分で文を作ります。2つの文を、**who / which / that** を使って1つの文にしましょう。

1. I have a neighbor. She is very kind and generous. → **I have a neighbor who is very kind and generous.**
2. This is the jacket. I bought it last year. → **This is the jacket that / which I bought last year.**
3. Emma is the classmate. She sat next to me in English class. → **Emma is the classmate who / that sat next to me in English class.**
4. It's a gadget. It helps you remember things. → **It's a gadget that / which helps you remember things.**
5. Daniel is the neighbor. He lived next to us when we were children. → **Daniel is the neighbor who / that lived next to us when we were children.**

### 文を完成させよう

**who**、**which**、**that** のいずれかを使って、次の文を完成させましょう。

6. I like people _____________________________________________ (どんな人が好きか書きましょう)
7. My favorite thing in my room is the _____________________________________________ that _____________________________________________
8. She's the kind of person _____________________________________________
