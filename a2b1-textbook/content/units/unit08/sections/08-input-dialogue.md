音声を聞きながら、Lily と Noah が同窓会（class reunion）で昔のクラスメートについて話す様子を読みましょう。

**Lily:** Hi, Noah! It's good to see you again.

**Noah:** Hi, Lily! It's been a long time. Do you remember everyone from our class?

**Lily:** Some people. Do you see the woman with curly hair, next to the tall man?

**Noah:** Yes, I see her. Who is she?

**Lily:** That's Emma. She's the classmate who sat next to me in English class.

**Noah:** Oh, I remember her now! Who is the man who is wearing the blue jacket?

**Lily:** That's Jack. He's the friend who was always late for school.

**Noah:** Oh, that's true! I remember that. And who is the woman with glasses and a necklace, next to the door?

**Lily:** That's Sophie. She's the friend who helped me with English.

**Noah:** She is very confident now. Is she still funny?

**Lily:** Yes! She's the kind of person who is always funny.

**Noah:** Is that the same bag that you had in school?

**Lily:** No, this is new. It's the same brand that I always buy, though.

**Noah:** It's a nice bag. Who is the quiet man with a beard, next to the food?

**Lily:** That's Daniel. He's the neighbor who lived next to us when we were children.

**Noah:** Small world! He is very serious, I think.

**Lily:** He's very shy, but he's also kind and generous.

**Noah:** I'd like to say hi. Can you introduce me?

**Lily:** Okay! Let's go and talk to him.

---

### 内容チェック

1. Emma is the classmate who ( sat next to Lily in English class / lived next to Lily's family ).
2. Jack is the friend who was ( always late for school / always very quiet ).
3. Daniel is the neighbor who ( taught Lily English / lived next to Lily's family ).
