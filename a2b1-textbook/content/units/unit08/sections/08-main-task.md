  scenario: >
    The learner is playing a "Guess Who" style guessing game with an AI
    partner named Robin at the class reunion. The learner has a page
    showing six people with short descriptions (appearance, personality,
    job). Robin has secretly chosen one specific person from the list but
    will not say the name unless the learner makes a correct final guess.
    The learner must ask questions using relative clauses (who / that) to
    narrow down which person Robin is thinking of.
  ai_role: >
    You are Robin, a friendly classmate at the reunion. Before the
    conversation starts, silently choose one person from the six people the
    learner can see and keep that choice consistent for the whole session.
    Answer the learner's yes/no and short questions honestly and briefly,
    using only vocabulary the learner has already studied (see
    known_vocab_cap). Do not say the chosen person's name until the learner
    makes a final guess with a full sentence. If a question is unclear, ask
    a short clarification question instead of guessing what was meant. If
    the learner makes a small grammar mistake, recast it naturally in your
    next turn rather than correcting it directly.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask at least three questions using a relative clause with "who" or
    "that" (for example, "Is it the person who has curly hair?") to figure

    one full sentence using a relative clause (for example, "I think it's
    the person who is wearing the black jacket.").

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

下の6人は、あなたの同窓会に来ている人たちです。Robin（AIの友だち）は、この中の1人をひそかに選んでいます。誰を選んだかは教えてくれません。あなたは質問して当てましょう。

| 名前 | 見た目 | 性格 | 仕事 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mia | curly hair, wears glasses | friendly and talkative | teacher |
| Leo | tall, short straight hair | quiet and honest | engineer |
| Ana | long hair, wears a necklace | confident and funny | artist |
| Tom | has a beard, wears a black jacket | kind and generous | doctor |
| Kate | short hair, wears a red scarf | shy but creative | musician |
| Sam | bald, wears a green sweater | serious and patient | scientist |

1. 表を見て、6人それぞれについて、**who** を使った説明の文を1つ頭の中で作ってみましょう（例：*Mia is the person who wears glasses.*）。
2. 次の表現を最低3回使うことを目標にします。

   - **Is it the person who 〜?** / **Is it the person that 〜?**（見た目・性格・仕事について質問する）
   - 最後の答え：**I think it's the person who / that 〜.**

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/8` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Robin に質問しながら、誰のことを考えているか当てましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

家族に、上の表の中から1人をひそかに選んでもらいましょう（表を見せずに）。**who / that** を使った質問を5つ以上書き、それに対する答えを予想してみましょう。

```
Q1:
Q2:
Q3:
Q4:
Q5:
Final guess:
```
