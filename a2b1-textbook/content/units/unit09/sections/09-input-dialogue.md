音声を聞きながら、Aoi と Jake が Jake の体調について話す様子を読みましょう。

**Aoi:** Hi Jake. You don't look very well today.

**Jake:** Yeah, I don't feel well. I have a headache and a sore throat.

**Aoi:** Oh no. Do you have a fever?

**Jake:** I think so. I feel really hot.

**Aoi:** You should go home and rest.

**Jake:** But I have to go to school this afternoon.

**Aoi:** You shouldn't go to school if you're sick. You should ask the teacher first.

**Jake:** That's true. I'll talk to her after school.

**Aoi:** Do you have any medicine?

**Jake:** No, I don't have anything with me.

**Aoi:** There is a pharmacy next to the station. You should ask the pharmacist for something.

**Jake:** Good idea. Do I have to make an appointment to see a doctor?

**Aoi:** Not for a cough and a headache. You should just get some rest.

**Jake:** Okay. I'll go to the pharmacy after school, then.

**Aoi:** And you shouldn't come to school tomorrow if you still have a fever.

**Jake:** Right. I don't want to make other people sick, too.

**Aoi:** That's true. Just call me and tell me how you feel.

**Jake:** Thanks, Aoi. I'll call you this evening.

**Aoi:** Get well soon!

**Jake:** Thanks!

---

### 内容チェック

1. Jake has a headache and a ( sore throat / stomachache ).
2. Aoi tells Jake he ( should / shouldn't ) go to school if he's sick.
3. Jake is going to go to the ( hospital / pharmacy ) after school.
