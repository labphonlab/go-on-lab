  scenario: >
    The learner visits a pharmacy because they don't feel well. The AI plays
    a pharmacist named Ms. Lee who knows which medicines are available, how
    to take them, and when the learner should see a doctor instead; the
    learner does not know these details and must describe their symptoms and
    ask questions to get clear advice.
  ai_role: >
    You are Ms. Lee, a friendly pharmacist. You know the store has cold
    medicine, painkillers, and cough medicine, but no prescription medicine
    (the learner has to see a doctor for that). You know painkillers should
    be taken with food and should not be taken more than three times a day.
    Do not reveal this information unless the learner asks a relevant
    question. Respond only using vocabulary the learner has already studied
    (see known_vocab_cap). Use natural, short A2-B1 level turns.
  learner_goal: >
    Describe your symptoms to Ms. Lee and ask questions to find out what
    medicine you should take and how to take it. Ask at least one question
    using "have to" or "don't have to" to find out whether you need to see a
    doctor. Use "should" or "shouldn't" at least once to check her advice.
  known_vocab_cap: unit_09

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. 今日の自分の症状を1つか2つ考えましょう（例：頭痛、のどの痛み、せき など）。
2. Ms. Lee（AI の薬剤師）は、あなたが知らない情報（どの薬が使えるか、飲み方、医者に行く必要があるか）を持っています。質問して聞き出しましょう。
3. 次の表現を最低1回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - 症状を伝える（例：*I have a headache and a sore throat.*）
   - **have to / don't have to** を使って、医者に行く必要があるか尋ねる（例：*Do I have to see a doctor?*）
   - **should / shouldn't** を使って、アドバイスを確認する（例：*Should I take this with food?*）

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/9` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Ms. Lee と英語でやり取りしながら、自分の症状に合ったアドバイスをもらいましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

薬剤師役を自分で想像し、薬局でのやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。**should / shouldn't**、**have to / don't have to** を必ず使ってください。
