音声を聞きながら、Leo が Mia に、昨夜家の外で何が起きたのかを尋ねる様子を読みましょう。

**Leo:** Hey Mia, I heard something happened outside your house last night!

**Mia:** Yes! It was so strange. I was watching TV at nine o'clock when I heard a loud noise outside.

**Leo:** What was the noise?

**Mia:** I think it was a crash. I looked out the window, and I saw a man on a bike. He crashed into a parked car.

**Leo:** Was he hurt?

**Mia:** No, he was okay. But while I was watching, one neighbor ran outside to help him.

**Leo:** What were you doing before you heard the noise?

**Mia:** I was just sitting on the sofa, eating popcorn. My phone was charging on the table.

**Leo:** Did the man on the bike say anything?

**Mia:** Yes. He said he was riding too fast because it started raining, and he wanted to get home quickly.

**Leo:** Was the car owner angry?

**Mia:** A little upset, but not too angry. While they were talking, a car alarm suddenly went off!

**Leo:** Oh! That's wild. What happened next?

**Mia:** The man said sorry to the car owner, and they talked for a while.

**Leo:** Then what?

**Mia:** Then everyone went inside again.

**Leo:** What a story! I'm happy nobody got hurt.

**Mia:** Me too! It was a very exciting night.

---

### 内容チェック

1. Mia was ( watching TV / cooking dinner ) when she heard the noise.
2. The man on the bike crashed into a ( parked car / shop window ).
3. The noise happened because ( a delivery arrived / a man crashed his bike ).
