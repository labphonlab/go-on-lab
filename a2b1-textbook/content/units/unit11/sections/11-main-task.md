  scenario: >
    The learner is a local reporter. An AI witness named Jamie saw a small
    accident (a bike crash) near the train station last night and knows
    exactly what was happening at the time and what happened at the moment
    of the crash. The learner does not know these details and must ask
    questions to reconstruct the full story for a short news report.
  ai_role: >
    You are Jamie, a calm and friendly witness who saw a bike accident near
    the station last night. You know: (1) what you were doing at the time
    (waiting for a bus, watching people), (2) what was happening in the
    background just before the crash (it was raining, a delivery rider was
    riding too fast), (3) the exact moment of the crash (the rider crashed
    into a parked car), and (4) what happened immediately after (a neighbor
    helped, nobody was badly injured, the police arrived a few minutes
    later). Do not reveal these details unless the learner asks a relevant
    question. Respond only using vocabulary the learner has already studied
    (see known_vocab_cap). Use natural, short B1-level turns, and give the
    learner a chance to notice when they should ask "what were you doing"
    or "what happened".
  learner_goal: >
    Interview Jamie to find out what was happening in the background before
    the accident and what the main event was. Use "was/were + -ing" to ask
    about the background (for example, "What were you doing when it
    happened?") and past simple to ask about the main event (for example,
    "What happened next?"). Use "while" or "when" at least once while
    summarizing the story back to Jamie.
  known_vocab_cap: unit_11
  recast: true
  clarification_requests: true
  eval_rubric: >
    1) Did the learner ask at least one background question using "was/were
    + -ing" (e.g. "What were you doing?", "Was it raining?")? 2) Did the
    learner ask at least one main-event question using past simple (e.g.
    "What happened?", "Did anyone get hurt?")? 3) Did the learner use
    "while" or "when" at least once to connect background and main event
    when summarizing the story? 4) Was the final summary clear about both
    the background and the main event, in a logical order?

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. あなたは地元の記者です。Jamie（AI の目撃者）は、駅の近くで昨夜起きた小さな事故を見ました。あなたはまだ詳しいことを知りません。
2. 次の2種類の質問を、それぞれ最低1回は使うことを目標にします。

   - **背景を尋ねる質問**（was/were + -ing を使う）：*What were you doing when it happened? Was it raining?*
   - **出来事を尋ねる質問**（普通の過去形を使う）：*What happened? Did anyone get hurt?*

3. インタビューが終わったら、聞いた内容を **while** か **when** を使って1つの文にまとめてみましょう。（例：*While Jamie was waiting for the bus, a rider crashed into a parked car.*）

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/11` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Jamie に英語でインタビューしながら、事故の全体像を組み立てましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

目撃者役を自分で想像し、インタビューのやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。**was/were + -ing** の質問と、**while / when** を使った要約文を必ず含めてください。
