roleplay:
  scenario: >
    The learner and an AI friend named Kai are planning what to do this
    weekend. Kai has already checked the weather forecast and knows Kai's
    own work schedule for Saturday and Sunday; the learner does not know
    these details and must ask questions to build a weekend plan that
    depends on the weather and on whether Kai has free time.
  ai_role: >
    You are Kai, a friendly peer of the learner. You know the weekend
    forecast (rain in the morning on Saturday, clearing up by the
    afternoon; sunny all day Sunday) and you know that if you don't finish
    a work report today, you'll have to work overtime on Saturday morning.
    Do not reveal this information unless the learner asks a relevant
    question. Respond only using vocabulary the learner has already
    studied (see known_vocab_cap). Use natural, short A2-B1 level turns,
    and include at least one "if..., will..." sentence yourself as a model.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask Kai questions to find out about the weather forecast and Kai's
    schedule this weekend. Then make a weekend plan using "if + present
    simple, ... will..." to show how the plan depends on those conditions
    (for example, "If it's sunny on Sunday, we'll go hiking. If you have
    to work Saturday morning, we'll meet in the afternoon instead.").
  known_vocab_cap: unit_12
  recast: true
  clarification_requests: true
  eval_rubric: >
    1) Did the learner ask at least two questions to find out information
    Kai had (weather / schedule)? 2) Did the learner produce at least two
    "if + present simple, ... will..." sentences? 3) Did the learner keep
    the verb in the if-clause in the present tense (not "if it will
    rain")? 4) Was the final plan clear and did it logically depend on the
    conditions discussed with Kai?

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. あなたが今週末やりたいことを1つ考えましょう（例：hiking、picnic、movie など）。
2. Kai（AI の友だち）は、あなたが知らない情報（天気予報・仕事の予定）を持っています。質問して聞き出しましょう。
3. 次の表現を最低1回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - 天気や予定を尋ねる質問（例：*What's the weather forecast for Saturday? Do you have to work this weekend?*）
   - **if + 現在形, ... will 〜** で、条件によって計画が変わることを伝える（例：*If it's sunny, we'll go hiking. If it rains, we'll do something indoor instead.*）

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/12` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Kai と英語でやり取りしながら、条件によって変わる週末の計画を完成させましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

友だち役を自分で想像し、テキストメッセージのやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。**if + 現在形, ... will 〜** の文を、少なくとも2回使ってください。
