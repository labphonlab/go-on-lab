音声を聞きながら、Nina が新しいフィットネスセンターの受付で Oscar にいろいろ質問する様子を読みましょう。

**Nina:** Excuse me, could you tell me what time you open on Saturday?

**Oscar:** Sure! We open at eight in the morning on Saturday.

**Nina:** Great, thanks. Do you know if there's a parking lot nearby?

**Oscar:** Yes, there's a parking lot right next to the building. It's free.

**Nina:** That's helpful. Could you tell me how much a monthly membership is?

**Oscar:** A monthly membership is thirty dollars. There's also a special discount if you're a student.

**Nina:** Nice! Do you know whether I need to book a class in advance?

**Oscar:** Not usually. But the swimming class is very popular, so I recommend booking in advance.

**Nina:** Okay, I'll do that. Could you tell me where the changing room is?

**Oscar:** It's down this hallway, next to the pool entrance. You'll see a sign.

**Nina:** Okay. Do you know if there's an elevator? I have a lot to carry today.

**Oscar:** Yes, there's an elevator right next to the reception desk.

**Nina:** Thank you so much for your help!

**Oscar:** You're welcome! Here is a brochure with more information. Could you fill out this form if you'd like to sign up?

**Nina:** Sure. Could you tell me where I should sign?

**Oscar:** Just sign at the bottom, next to today's date.

**Nina:** Got it. Thanks again!

**Oscar:** No problem. Have a great time at the center!

---

### 内容チェック

1. The center opens at eight in the morning on ( Saturday / Sunday ).
2. A monthly membership costs ( twenty / thirty ) dollars.
3. Nina should sign the form near today's ( date / name ).
