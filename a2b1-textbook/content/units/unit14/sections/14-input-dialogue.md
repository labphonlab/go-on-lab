音声を聞きながら、Mei と Leo がカフェでコーヒーとカップについて話す様子を読みましょう。

**Leo:** This coffee is really good. Where is it from?

**Mei:** It's from Brazil, I think. A lot of coffee is grown there.

**Leo:** Oh, interesting! How is it made? I mean, from the farm to the cup.

**Mei:** First, the coffee beans are picked by hand.

**Leo:** By hand? That sounds difficult.

**Mei:** Yes, it is. After that, the beans are dried in the sun.

**Leo:** And then?

**Mei:** Then they're moved to a factory. There, they're roasted and packed into a bag.

**Leo:** I didn't know that. So the beans are roasted before they're shipped?

**Mei:** Yes, that's right. And after that, they're sold to a cafe like this one.

**Leo:** Interesting. This cafe is in an old building, right?

**Mei:** Yes! It was built in 1920.

**Leo:** Oh, who built it?

**Mei:** I don't know, but it was used as a small factory before it was a cafe.

**Leo:** That's cool. This building is beautiful.

**Mei:** Me too. Everything here is made of local wood.

**Leo:** Nice. I feel like everything here is natural.

**Mei:** Oh, and this cup is made in Japan.

**Leo:** Really? By a local company?

**Mei:** Yes, a small company in Kyoto made it by hand.

**Leo:** That's so interesting. I want to see that factory one day.

**Mei:** Me too! We can go there soon.

---

### 内容チェック

1. The coffee beans are dried in ( the factory / the sun ).
2. The building was built in ( 1920 / 2020 ).
3. The cup is made in ( Brazil / Japan ).
