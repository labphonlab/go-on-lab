Nina went to a cafe to ask about coffee. She wanted to know how it was made.

"Could you tell me where the coffee beans come from?" she asked the waiter.

"Sure," he said. "The beans are grown on a farm, and they are shipped here. Then they are roasted and packed by a machine."

While Nina was waiting for her coffee, she noticed something strange outside. A bike crashed into a parked car!

"What happened?" she asked a man next to her.

"The person on the bike was riding too fast," the man said. "He didn't see the car."

Nina felt bad, but nobody was hurt.

After that, Nina checked the weather forecast on her phone.

"If it's sunny this weekend, I'll go hiking," she thought. "But if it's cloudy, I'll stay home and read a book instead."

She also remembered her appointment.

"Do you know if the gym is open on Sunday?" she asked the waiter.

"I'm not sure," he said, "but you could call and ask."

Nina finished her coffee and left. She thought about her weekend plan.
