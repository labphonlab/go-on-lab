今度は自分で文を作ります。（　）の中の動詞を、正しい形（**to + 動詞の原形** または **動詞 + -ing**）に変えて書きましょう。

1. I want ( go ) to a new country next year. → **to go**
2. My brother enjoys ( play ) the guitar in his free room. → **playing**
3. We decided ( study ) English together every weekend. → **to study**
4. She avoids ( use ) her phone before bed. → **using**
5. I hope ( become ) a good teacher someday. → **to become**
6. He plans ( apply ) for a new job next month. → **to apply**
7. They promised ( finish ) the project by Friday. → **to finish**
8. Do you keep ( practice ) even when it's hard? → **practicing**

### 自分の希望・目標について書こう

次の文を、あなた自身について完成させましょう。動詞の形に気をつけてください。

10. I hope to _____________________________________________ someday.
11. I enjoy _____________________________________________.
12. I try to avoid _____________________________________________.
