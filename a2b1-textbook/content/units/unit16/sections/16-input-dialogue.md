音声を聞きながら、Daniel と Aoi が将来の希望について話す様子を読みましょう。

**Daniel:** Hi Aoi! Do you have a plan for the future?

**Aoi:** Hi Daniel! Yes, I do. I want to become a nurse.

**Daniel:** That's great! Why do you want to do that?

**Aoi:** I enjoy helping people, and I hope to work at a hospital close to my home.

**Daniel:** Nice. Do you plan to study at university first?

**Aoi:** Yes, I already decided to apply next year. What about you? Do you have a goal?

**Daniel:** I dream of starting my own business someday. I love cooking, so I want to open a small restaurant.

**Aoi:** Oh, that sounds exciting! Have you started to save money for it?

**Daniel:** Yes, I try to save some money every month. I also plan to travel and learn about food in another country first.

**Aoi:** That's a good idea. Do you enjoy traveling?

**Daniel:** I do! I really enjoy meeting new people and trying new food. But I avoid spending too much money when I travel.

**Aoi:** I know you'll do it. I hope you can achieve your dream.

**Daniel:** Thanks! And I hope you become a great nurse. Let's both work to reach our goal.

**Aoi:** Yes, let's! I promise to tell you when I finish my exam.

**Daniel:** I'll be waiting. Good luck, Aoi!

**Aoi:** Thanks, Daniel. Good luck to you too!

---

### 内容チェック

1. Aoi wants to become a ( nurse / teacher ).
2. Daniel dreams of starting his own ( business / school ) someday.
3. Before that, Daniel plans to ( travel / study ) and try food from another country.
