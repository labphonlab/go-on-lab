  scenario: >
    The learner is talking with Rio, an old friend who now works as a career
    advisor. Rio knows about three different programs (a volunteer program,
    a study-abroad program, and a part-time job with training) that could
    help the learner reach their goal, including details like location,
    length, and cost. The learner does not know these details and must ask
    questions to find the program that best matches their own hopes and
    plans. Rio is also curious about the learner's future and asks questions
    in return.
  ai_role: >
    You are Rio, a friendly career advisor and old friend of the learner.
    You know about three programs the learner does not know about: (1) a
    volunteer program abroad, (2) a study-abroad program at a university,
    and (3) a part-time job with on-the-job training. Each has different
    length, location, and cost. Do not list all three at once -- reveal
    details only when the learner asks a relevant question, and ask the
    learner questions about their own hopes and plans in return (for
    example, "What do you hope to do in five years?" or "Do you enjoy
    working with people?"). When the learner makes a small grammar mistake
    (for example, "I want traveling" or "I enjoy to cook"), do not correct
    them directly -- restate their idea naturally using the correct form
    (recast), then continue the conversation. If the learner's meaning is
    unclear, ask a short clarification question instead of guessing.
    Respond only using vocabulary the learner has already studied (see
    known_vocab_cap). Use natural, short B1-level turns.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask Rio questions to find out about the three programs and choose the
    one that best fits your hopes and goals. Tell Rio about your own future
    using "want to / hope to / plan to / decide to", and talk about what you
    enjoy or want to avoid using "enjoy -ing / avoid -ing".
  known_vocab_cap: unit_16
  recast: true

  eval_rubric: >

    Did the learner ask at least two questions to get information Rio had
    about the programs? 4) Did the learner reach a clear final choice and
    explain why it matches their goal?

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. あなたが将来やってみたいことを1つ考えましょう（例：海外で働く、資格を取る、自分の店を持つなど）。
2. Rio（AI の友だち）は、あなたの目標に役立つかもしれないプログラムの情報を持っています。質問して聞き出しましょう。
3. 次の表現を最低1回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - **want to / hope to / plan to / decide to** で自分の希望や予定を伝える
   - **enjoy -ing / avoid -ing** で好きなこと・避けたいことを伝える
   - プログラムについて尋ねる質問（例：*What is it like? How long is it? Where is it?*）

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/16` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Rio と英語でやり取りしながら、自分に合うプログラムを見つけましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

Rio 役を自分で想像し、チャットのやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。**want to / hope to / plan to / decide to** と **enjoy -ing / avoid -ing** を必ず使ってください。
