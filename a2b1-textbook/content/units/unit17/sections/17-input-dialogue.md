音声を聞きながら、Leo と Yuki が「猫と犬、どちらが良いペットか」について意見を交換する様子を読みましょう。

**Leo:** Hi, Yuki! Can I ask you something?

**Yuki:** Sure, ask me.

**Leo:** Which is a better pet, a cat or a dog?

**Yuki:** That's easy! In my opinion, dogs are the best pets. They're always happy to see you, and they love to play.

**Leo:** I don't think so. I think cats are better. They're calm, and they don't need a walk every day.

**Yuki:** I disagree. Dogs are more loyal, and they're a lot of fun, too.

**Leo:** That's a good point. However, cats are more independent. You can leave them alone during the day.

**Yuki:** I see your point, but I still like dogs better. Do you have a pet?

**Leo:** Yes, I have a cat. Her name is Momo. She's calm and friendly.

**Yuki:** That sounds nice! I really like pets.

**Leo:** So do I! That's why I have three cats at home, actually.

**Yuki:** Three? That's a lot! I don't like too many pets in one house.

**Leo:** Neither do I, but my sister wanted them, so we still have them.

**Yuki:** I guess it's different for everyone. Everyone has a different opinion.

**Leo:** Exactly! Honestly, both cats and dogs make great pets.

**Yuki:** I agree completely. There's no wrong answer here.

**Leo:** True! Let's agree to disagree and get some food.

**Yuki:** Sounds good to me!

---

### 内容チェック

1. Yuki thinks ( cats / dogs ) make the best pets.
2. Leo has ( one cat / three cats ) at home.
3. In the end, Leo and Yuki decide to ( agree to disagree / keep arguing ).
