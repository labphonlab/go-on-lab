音声を聞きながら、Maya が Leo に、先週末 Osaka への旅行で起きたことを順番に話す様子を読みましょう。

**Leo:** Hey Maya! How was your trip to Osaka last weekend?

**Maya:** It's an interesting story! I'll tell you what happened.

**Leo:** Go on!

**Maya:** Okay. At first, everything was good. I was walking to the station when I noticed I didn't have my ticket.

**Leo:** Oh no! What did you do?

**Maya:** I looked in my bag, but it wasn't there. I felt so confused. Then I realized — I had left it on the table at home.

**Leo:** That's a big mistake!

**Maya:** I know. So I decided to buy a new one. But when I got to the platform, there was a big crowd, and it was difficult to hear the announcement.

**Leo:** What happened next?

**Maya:** I asked a stranger next to me what the announcement said. He told me the train was at a different platform.

**Leo:** Did you make it in time?

**Maya:** I ran, and I just caught it! I was so relieved. While I was sitting on the train, the same man sat next to me, and we started talking.

**Leo:** What did he say?

**Maya:** He said he was going to Osaka too, to see a friend. He mentioned that he goes there every year.

**Leo:** That's great!

**Maya:** Yes! I was still nervous about being late, but he promised me the train was fast. Eventually, it felt like we knew each other for a long time.

**Leo:** So it turned out great after all!

**Maya:** Really! I missed my train and lost my ticket, but it turned out great.

**Leo:** That's a great story, Maya!

---

### 内容チェック

1. Maya couldn't find her ( ticket / phone ) at home.
2. The stranger told Maya that the train was at a ( different platform / different station ).
3. In the end, Maya's trip ( turned out great / turned out badly ).
