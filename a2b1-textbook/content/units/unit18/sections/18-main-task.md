  scenario: >
    The learner's friend Nina heard something happened to their mutual friend
    Kenji over the weekend, but Nina was not there herself -- she only knows
    what Kenji told her afterward. The learner must ask Nina questions to
    find out what happened to Kenji, in order, then retell the whole story
    back to Nina in a clear sequence, and report at least one thing Nina
    told them using reported speech.
  ai_role: >
    You are Nina, a friendly peer of the learner. You heard secondhand from
    Kenji that he went hiking on Saturday, got lost on the trail while it
    was getting dark, felt nervous, then met a stranger who was also lost,
    and together they found the way back. Kenji told you he arrived home
    very late but was fine in the end. Reveal these details only when the
    learner asks a relevant question, and reveal them one piece at a time,
    not all at once. When you share what Kenji told you, use reporting
    language yourself (e.g. "Kenji told me he was nervous"). Respond only
    using vocabulary the learner has already studied (see known_vocab_cap).
    Use natural, short B1 level turns.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask Nina at least three or four questions to piece together what
    happened to Kenji, using past simple and past continuous. Then retell
    the story back to Nina in a clear sequence, using words like "first",
    "then", "after that", "eventually", and "in the end". Finally, report
    at least one thing Nina told you, using "Nina said (that)..." or
    "Nina told me (that)...".
  known_vocab_cap: unit_18
  recast: true
  clarification_requests: true
  eval_rubric: >
    1) Did the learner ask at least three relevant questions to gather the

    continuous at least once to describe background action (e.g. "he was
    walking", "it was getting dark")? 4) Did the learner report at least
    one thing Nina said, using reported speech (said that / told me that)?

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. Nina（AI の友だち）は、Kenji に起きたことを直接見ていません。Kenji から聞いた話しか知りません。あなたは質問して、少しずつ情報を聞き出す必要があります。
2. 次の表現を最低1回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - 出来事を尋ねる質問（例：*What happened to Kenji? Where was he when it happened?*）
   - **過去進行形** で背景を語る（例：*He was walking on the trail when...*）
   - 話の順序を示す語（**first**, **then**, **after that**, **eventually**, **in the end**）
   - **said / told me** を使って、Nina が言ったことを伝える

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/18` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Nina に質問しながら Kenji に起きたことを聞き出し、最後に話を順番にまとめて Nina に語り返しましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。

**アプリが使えない場合の代替タスク（紙のみ）**

Nina 役を自分で想像し、質問と答えのやり取りを6〜8往復、英語で書いてみましょう。そのあと、聞き出した話を3〜4文でまとめ、**said** または **told me** を必ず1回使ってください。
