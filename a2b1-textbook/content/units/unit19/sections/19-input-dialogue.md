音声を聞きながら、Emi と Jack が Jack の旅行中のトラブルについて話す様子を読みましょう。

**Emi:** Hi Jack! How was your trip to Italy?

**Jack:** Not so good! I had a big problem at the airport.

**Emi:** Oh no, what happened?

**Jack:** My flight connection was delayed, and then I missed it.

**Emi:** That's terrible! What did you do?

**Jack:** I talked to the airline staff at the gate, but there was no other flight until the next morning.

**Emi:** That's so stressful. What about a hotel?

**Jack:** The airline gave me a hotel room for the night, but it was really late when I got there.

**Emi:** I'm sorry to hear that. Was that the only problem?

**Jack:** No! When I finally arrived in Rome, my suitcase was missing.

**Emi:** Oh no! What did you do?

**Jack:** I went to the lost and found desk and made a claim. They said they would send it to my hotel.

**Emi:** Did they find it?

**Jack:** Yes, but not until the day after tomorrow! I had to buy new clothes.

**Emi:** If I lost my suitcase like that, I would be so upset.

**Jack:** I know. Now I think I should have packed some clothes in my carry-on bag.

**Emi:** That's a good idea. I always keep my passport and some money in mine.

**Jack:** Yeah, I should not have put everything in one suitcase.

**Emi:** What about your passport? Did you have a problem with that?

**Jack:** No, that was okay. But if I lost my passport, I would go to the embassy immediately.

**Emi:** That's smart. Did you get any money back for the delay?

**Jack:** I complained to the airline, and they gave me some money back.

**Emi:** But you got something back. If I were you, I would also buy travel insurance next time.

**Jack:** Good point. I think I learned a lot from this trip.

**Emi:** Every problem can teach us something. Are you going to travel again soon?

**Jack:** Yes, next month. I hope my next trip is better!

---

### 内容チェック

1. Jack's flight connection was delayed, and he ( missed it / cancelled it ).
2. When Jack arrived in Rome, his suitcase was ( missing / stolen ).
3. Jack thinks he should have ( packed some clothes in his carry-on bag / bought a new suitcase ).
