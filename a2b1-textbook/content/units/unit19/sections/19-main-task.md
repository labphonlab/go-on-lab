  scenario: >
    The learner's flight has just been cancelled, and they are talking to an
    AI airline agent named Alex at the help desk. Alex knows the rebooking
    options, the hotel policy, and the refund policy, but the learner does
    not know these details and must ask questions to find out what would
    happen in different hypothetical situations. The learner should also
    explain one mistake they made before the trip (for example, not
    checking the flight status, or not buying travel insurance) using
    should have / should not have.
  ai_role: >
    You are Alex, a calm and helpful airline agent. You know that if the
    learner's flight is cancelled, the airline would offer a seat on the
    next available flight tomorrow morning; if the wait is longer than six
    hours, the airline would also give a free hotel room; and if the
    learner already bought travel insurance, they would get a full refund
    for extra costs (without insurance, only a partial refund). Do not
    reveal this information unless the learner asks a relevant question.
    When the learner explains a past mistake, respond naturally and
    supportively (for example, agree it would have helped, or gently note
    what they could do differently next time). Respond only using
    vocabulary the learner has already studied (see known_vocab_cap). Use
    natural, short A2-B1 level turns.
  learner_goal: >
    Ask Alex questions about what would happen in different hypothetical
    situations, using "if + past simple, would + verb" (for example, "If I
    wait more than six hours, would you give me a hotel room?"). Then
    explain one mistake you made before the trip using "should have" or
    "should not have" (for example, "I should have bought travel
    insurance.").
  known_vocab_cap: unit_19
  recast: true
  clarification_requests: true
  eval_rubric: >
    1) Did the learner ask at least two questions using "if + past simple,
    would + verb" to find out what Alex would do in a hypothetical

    explain at least one past mistake? 3) Did the learner ask enough
    questions to find the key information Alex had (rebooking, hotel,
    refund)? 4) Was the final plan or explanation clear and understandable?

**紙面での準備（アプリを使う前に）**

1. 想像してください：あなたの飛行機がキャンセルになり、空港のヘルプデスクにいます。Alex（AI の航空会社スタッフ）は、あなたが知らない情報（次の便・ホテル・返金のルール）を持っています。質問して聞き出しましょう。
2. 次の表現を最低1回ずつ使うことを目標にします。

   - **if + 過去形, would 〜** で、いろいろな場合にどうなるかを尋ねる質問（例：*If I wait more than six hours, would you give me a hotel room?*）
   - **should have / should not have** で、旅行前に自分がした失敗を説明する（例：*I should have bought travel insurance.*）

**アプリでのロールプレイ**

QR コード（このユニットの先頭）または `learn.goonresearch.jp/u/19` からアプリを開き、"Main Task: Roleplay" を選んでください。Alex と英語でやり取りしながら、次の便やホテル、返金についての情報を集め、自分の失敗についても話してみましょう。会話が終わると、AI が達成度を確認してくれます。
