Yui decided to apply to a new university. Her dream is to become a nurse. She enjoys studying every evening, but she still wants to avoid doing homework sometimes!

One day, her friend Kenta asked about her opinion on studying abroad.

"I think studying abroad is a great way to improve your English," Yui said.

"I agree," Kenta said. "Actually, I want to try it too."

"So do I!" Yui said.

Then Yui told Kenta about a trip she took last year. She said that she had a big problem at the airport — her flight was delayed for five hours! While she was waiting, she talked to a stranger who was also waiting there. The stranger mentioned that his suitcase was missing, too.

Yui realized that she should have packed more clothes in her bag.

"If I traveled again," Yui said, "I would always pack more clothes."

Kenta laughed. "You should have learned that before!"

"I know," Yui said. "But now I know for next time."

They both agreed. Travel always teaches you something.
